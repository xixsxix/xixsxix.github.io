# Prompt_숏폼 배경 이미지 생성

> [!note]
> You are a professional background designer for short-form news videos.
> 
> Create vertical background images optimized for 1440x2560 (9:16) short-form video production.
> 
> [Core Purpose]
> These backgrounds will be used for a fast-paced news-style YouTube Shorts channel called "FocusONE".
> The design must support overlay text, headlines, and subtitles clearly.
> 
> [Output Requirement - VERY IMPORTANT]
> - Generate exactly 8 images total
> - Each image must be shown individually (NOT combined, NOT in a grid, NOT side-by-side)
> - Each image must be a standalone output
> - Do NOT merge multiple designs into one image
> 
> ---
> 
> [Style Distribution]
> Create 4 styles, with 2 variations per style:
> 
> ---
> 
> [Style 1: News / Standard]
> Mood: serious, trustworthy, modern
> Color: dark background with blue accents
> Lighting: subtle glow, clean gradients
> Design:
> - Clear empty space in the center for large headline text
> - Top area should function as a title zone
> - Bottom area must remain clean for subtitles
> - Include subtle tech lines, grids, or abstract data patterns
> 
> ---
> 
> [Style 2: Breaking / Urgent]
> Mood: urgent, intense, high attention
> Color: dark with red highlights
> Lighting: strong contrast, alert-style glow
> Design:
> - Visual tension (diagonal lines, motion blur, alert bars)
> - Strong framing for headline text
> - Keep readability high despite intensity
> 
> ---
> 
> [Style 3: Entertainment / Trend]
> Mood: energetic, trendy, slightly lighter
> Color: purple, blue, or gradient tones
> Lighting: soft glow, modern, slightly vibrant
> Design:
> - More dynamic composition
> - Must preserve text readability
> - Avoid clutter in center
> 
> ---
> 
> [Style 4: Neutral / Backup]
> Mood: minimal, flexible, universal
> Color: neutral dark tones (gray, navy, black)
> Lighting: very subtle
> Design:
> - Simple textures or soft gradients
> - Maximum usability for any topic
> - No strong visual bias
> 
> ---
> 
> [Global Rules - STRICT]
> - Resolution: 1440x2560
> - Vertical composition ONLY
> - DO NOT include any text, letters, numbers, or typography
> - DO NOT include any watermark, logo, or signature
> - DO NOT include any borders, frames, outlines, or edge decorations
> - Keep all edges clean and natural
> - Keep the center area clean for overlays
> - Avoid overly complex or noisy visuals
> - Design must feel professional and reusable
> 
> ---
> 
> [Composition Safety Rule]
> - Keep important visual elements away from the bottom edge
> - Leave safe margins for cropping (especially bottom area)
> 
> ---
> 
> [Important]
> All images must look like part of one consistent brand system.
> Maintain visual consistency across all 8 outputs.
> 
> Return the results as 8 separate, production-ready images.
>
