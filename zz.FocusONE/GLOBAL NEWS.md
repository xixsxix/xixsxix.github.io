# GLOBAL NEWS

```perplexity.ai
Collect major global news.

Time Scope:

- Yesterday Window:  
    Collect news published between 00:00–23:59 (KST) of the previous day
    
- Today Live Window:  
    Collect news published from 00:00 (KST) today up to the current time
    

Rules:

- Clearly separate yesterday and today items
    
- Do NOT mix the two time windows
    
- Avoid duplicate reporting of the same event across both windows
    

Scope:

- Cover politics, economy, society, AI/IT, entertainment, and gaming
    
- Focus on high-impact global events
    

Requirements:

- Provide 30–40 issues total
    
- Each category must include 6–10 items
    
- Avoid duplicate topics
    
- Focus on core events or structural changes
    

Source Strategy:

- Use diverse, high-credibility international sources (Reuters, AP, BBC, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ, etc.)
    

Output Format:

[Yesterday]

[Category]

Title:  
Core Content:  
(2–3 lines explaining cause → event → impact)

Why It Matters:  
(1 line)

---

[Today - Ongoing]

[Category]

Title:  
Core Content:  
(2–3 lines explaining situation)

Why It Matters:  
(1 line)

Rules:

- Do NOT include trivial or low-impact news
    
- Keep content factual and neutral
```
